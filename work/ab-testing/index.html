Feature / Interaction design

A/B testing for people who run experiments for a living

The platform needed built-in A/B testing, and the people using it run experiments for a living. Customers were faking holdouts with invisible variants, which caused bugs and support tickets. I designed the flow against 17 acceptance criteria and caught a metrics issue in a live session before release.

In one minute

SituationExperimentation UI is unforgiving: traffic has to sum correctly, control has to be unambiguous, and the interface has to work with one variant or twenty.
What I didSpecced every edge case with engineering before handoff, replaced the invisible-variant workaround with a real holdout, and reviewed the flow live with customers who test daily.

Decisions

Decision 01 / 04

Spec every edge case before engineering starts

I wrote the edge cases into the acceptance criteria: entry states for one versus many variants, traffic that doesn’t sum to 100 percent, the rule that the last variant can’t be deleted, and exactly what control means.

Why: so engineering could build without coming back with questions.

Decision 02 / 04

Replace a workaround with a real holdout

Customers needed a slice of traffic that sees nothing, and with no real feature they were faking one with invisible variants, causing scroll-lock bugs, CSS conflicts, and support tickets. I turned that workaround into requirements for a dedicated holdout that renders no creative at all, which keeps measurement clean.

Decision 03 / 04

Review it live with people who test for a living

Sessions with three enterprise customers who run experiments daily confirmed the core interactions and caught one subtle issue: a setting applied at the wrong level that would have corrupted the metrics. It went to engineering as a spike before it could ship.

One variable at a time: creative or settings, never both.Customer experimentation practice, validated in session
Decision 04 / 04

Let the split decide the default

Customers split on whether dismissal settings belong at the campaign or variant level. Instead of imposing one opinion, it became a configurable setting, with campaign level as the default because 2 of 3 customers preferred it.
